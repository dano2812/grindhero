# AI-assisted development and provenance

AI tools assisted with parts of this repository's game design, code, tests,
Unity project setup, and documentation. The game owner supplied product
requirements and decisions; the origin and degree of AI assistance vary by
file and change. This notice does not mean every file was generated entirely
by AI.

AI-assisted code, documentation, and content should be reviewed and validated
by maintainers before release. This notice is a repository-level transparency
statement; it is not legal advice and does not, by itself, establish compliance
with the EU AI Act or other laws.

## Regulatory context

The EU AI Act's transparency duties depend on the AI system, the provider's or
deployer's role, how the system is used, and the content it produces. Article
50 addresses, among other things, informing people when they interact directly
with certain AI systems, machine-readable marking of certain AI-generated
outputs, and disclosure for specified deepfakes and public-interest text.
These are not a general requirement to add a `Generated by AI` comment to every
source, documentation, configuration, or asset file. A source-code comment is
not a substitute for marking an AI system's outputs or providing disclosures
where the Act applies.

Using AI coding assistance to develop this repository is distinct from whether
the shipped game includes or exposes an AI system or publishes AI-generated
content. Review the intended product and distribution context separately
before release; seek qualified legal advice where needed.
